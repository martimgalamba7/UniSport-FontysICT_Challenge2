02

Core Platform Features

Everything built for seamless play.

Built for how students actually live and move. From verified campus peers to real-time spot reservations, UniSport removes every obstacle between you and your next workout.

01
Campus Verified

Student-Only Community

Play with confidence. UniSport is built exclusively for university students, creating a safe, trusted space where everyone on the court belongs to your campus. No random strangers, no commercial ads—just verified peers ready to play.

02
Tailored Matching

Your Sports, Your Skill Level

Whether you're hitting the gym, running 5K at sunset, rallying padel, or climbing, UniSport matches you with athletes on your wavelength. Choose your sports, set your intensity from casual fun to competitive sparring, and play with people who match your pace.

03
Live Campus Radar

Spontaneous Campus Feed

Got an unexpected hour free between classes? Open the live feed to see games and training sessions happening right around you. Filter by distance to find courts, running tracks, or gym buddies within a short walk or bike ride.

04
One-Tap Organizing

Host a Game in 30 Seconds

Need an extra player for 5v5 or a sparring partner for tennis? Post an activity in seconds: pick your sport, set the court, lock the exact player limit, and let fellow students fill the roster. Live spot tracking ensures your games are always full and never overbooked.

05
Social Transparency

Know Who's Playing Before You Go

Exercising with new people shouldn't feel intimidating. See who has already joined, check out who you'll be playing with, and show up with confidence. Spot counts and player rosters update instantly whenever someone joins or drops out—no missing players, no surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting, finding teammates, and organizing games.

Is UniSport completely free to use? +

Yes, UniSport is 100% free for students. Our goal is to break physical inactivity and make connecting for sports as seamless and spontaneous as sending a message.

How does match hosting work? +

Any verified student can post a match in under 30 seconds by picking a sport, choosing a location, selecting the time, and setting maximum participants. Nearby students will see the match in their feed and can claim open spots.

What if I'm a complete beginner in a sport? +

UniSport has skill tags on every activity ("Casual", "Beginner Friendly", "Intermediate", "Competitive"). You can join games matching your confidence level without feeling out of place.

Can I use UniSport on mobile? +

Absolutely. UniSport is built mobile-first as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can open it on Safari or Chrome on your phone, add it to your home screen, and access everything instantly.
