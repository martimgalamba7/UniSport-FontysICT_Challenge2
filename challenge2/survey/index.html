Sports Community Research Survey

This empirical research survey is conducted by Martim Ferreira Galamba as part of Challenge 2 (Semester 1, Block 2) within the HBO-ICT program at Fontys University of Applied Sciences in Eindhoven.

The primary objective of this study is to analyze sports participation patterns, communication friction, and logistical barriers among higher education students in Eindhoven. The insights gathered will directly inform the software requirements, user experience architecture, and relational database prototyping for UniSport, a student-centric platform designed to facilitate spontaneous match creation, club coordination, and local athletic networking.
