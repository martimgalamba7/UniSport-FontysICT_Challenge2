THE GAP BETWEEN WANTING TO PLAY AND ACTUALLY PLAYING.

FAULT 01

THE SOLO INERTIA

Moving to Eindhoven as a new student breaks your existing sports network. Over 68% of surveyed students skip physical exercise simply because finding a teammate feels like a logistical hurdle.

✕ RESULT: INACTIVITY & ISOLATION
FAULT 02

GROUP CHAT CHAOS

WhatsApp and Discord are messaging protocols, not activity managers. In unstructured chats with 80+ members, plans dissolve into endless polls, schedule clashes, and last-minute ghosting.

✕ RESULT: 200 MESSAGES, ZERO MATCHES

THE UNISPORT PROTOCOL

One central live map. Strict participant ceilings with instant roster locks. Broadcast a match in 30 seconds; let nearby students fill the spots in real time. No noise.

[05] // COMMON QUESTIONS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

01 // WHO IS ELIGIBLE TO JOIN UNISPORT? +

UniSport is engineered specifically for students enrolled in higher education in the Eindhoven region, including Fontys University of Applied Sciences, Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), and regional student sports clubs.

02 // HOW DOES REAL-TIME SPOT LOCKING PREVENT OVERBOOKING? +

Matches maintain a hard maximum spot capacity. When an athlete clicks "Join", a transactional Firestore state updates instantaneously for every connected student via real-time WebSocket listeners, locking the roster the millisecond capacity is hit.

03 // IS THE PLATFORM COMPLETELY FREE? +

Yes, 100% free of charge. Developed as an HBO-ICT academic innovation challenge to eradicate student isolation and physical inactivity in Eindhoven.

04 // WHAT STACK POWERS UNISPORT? +

Built on a modern reactive stack: TypeScript component architecture, Tailwind CSS industrial styling, Firebase Auth with student domain security, and Cloud Firestore for document synchronization.
