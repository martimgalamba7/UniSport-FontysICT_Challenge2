Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting, finding teammates, and organizing games.

Is UniSport completely free to use? +

Yes, UniSport is 100% free for students. Our goal is to break physical inactivity and make connecting for sports as seamless and spontaneous as sending a message.

How does match hosting work? +

Any verified student can post a match in under 30 seconds by picking a sport, choosing a location, selecting the time, and setting maximum participants. Nearby students will see the match in their feed and can claim open spots.

What if I'm a complete beginner in a sport? +

UniSport has skill tags on every activity ("Casual", "Beginner Friendly", "Intermediate", "Competitive"). You can join games matching your confidence level without feeling out of place.

Can I use UniSport on mobile? +

Absolutely. UniSport is built mobile-first as a Progressive Web App (PWA). You can open it on Safari or Chrome on your phone, add it to your home screen, and access everything instantly.
