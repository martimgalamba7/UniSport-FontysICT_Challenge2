The UniSport Ethos

Sports shouldn’t require a dozen WhatsApp messages or an established friend circle to happen.

When students move to Eindhoven to study at Fontys or TU/e, finding teammates, matching schedules, and booking sports courts can feel daunting. Many end up exercising alone or stopping entirely. UniSport turns Eindhoven into an open, inclusive sports pitch where anyone with shoes and energy can jump into a game.

Community Over Isolation

Eliminating the solo workout barrier. Whether you are an international student or a first-year commuter, you will never lack workout partners.

Spontaneous, Not Rigid

Student schedules are unpredictable. UniSport allows you to broadcast or discover matches happening today, between lectures, or over the weekend.

Inclusive Skill Matching

From relaxed fun to high-intensity competitive sparring. Filter by skill levels so everyone feels comfortable and challenged.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use the UniSport platform?

UniSport is designed primarily for higher education students living or studying in the Eindhoven region, including Fontys University of Applied Sciences, Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), and regional student sports clubs.

How does real-time spot management work?

Every match activity maintains a strict participant capacity. When a user clicks "Join", Cloud Firestore listeners immediately decrement available slots on every active user screen without requiring page refreshes, preventing overbooking.

Is UniSport free of charge for students?

Yes, completely free. UniSport is an academic community platform developed within Fontys School of ICT to combat physical inactivity and social friction in student life.

What technology stack powers UniSport?

The platform is built using a modern web stack: TypeScript logic, Tailwind CSS for Apple-grade responsive styling, Firebase Authentication for student access, and Cloud Firestore for real-time document synchronization.
